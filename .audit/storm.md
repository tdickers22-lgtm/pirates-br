## Findings

### 1. [MAJOR] Storm wall/ring/halo transforms only update when a snapshot arrives
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:2749
- Why: updateStormRing() — the only place stormRing vertices and stormWall/stormHalo position+scale are written (7328-7351) — is called solely from applySnapshot (2749). updateScene only rotates the wall and animates opacity (6278-6296). During a shrink the wall edge moves ~3-7 m/s; with the live snapshot cadence observed at ~1000-1400ms the wall visibly teleports meters at a time, and even at nominal 30Hz there is zero smoothing between updates.
- Fix: Call updateStormRing() from updateScene (after line 6280) every frame, and extrapolate locally: keep a client-side copy of storm center/radius, advance shrinkProgress by dt/this.state.storm.shrinkDuration between snapshots and lerp shrinkStartCenter/Radius→nextCenter/Radius exactly like StormSystem.update:61-63 does, snapping to server values in applySnapshot. positionAttr.needsUpdate is already set per call so per-frame cost is one 96-vertex write.

### 2. [MAJOR] Rain is a screen-space 2D canvas overlay, not world-space particles
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:10801
- Why: updateStormRainOverlay (10801-10836) strokes 65-485 pseudo-random 2D lines on a fullscreen DOM canvas created by setupStormWeatherOverlay (10690-10711, fixed div z-index 50). Drops scroll by ocean time only — they ignore camera yaw/pitch/velocity, never hit deck or sea, cast no splashes, and composite a full-screen 2D canvas over the WebGL canvas every frame at high quality (rainInterval=0 at effectScale>=0.85, 6285). This is exactly the arcade look reported.
- Fix: Replace the canvas with a THREE.Points (or instanced quad streaks) rain volume parented near the camera: ~2-4k vertical streaks in a 40m box recentered on renderer.camera each frame, falling at ~28m/s with wind shear from stormWeatherIntensity, wrapped modulo the box; density from computeStormRainIntensity, sized by effectScale. Drive it from the existing throttle block at 6284-6289 (replace updateStormRainOverlay call), keep #storm-lightning-flash div, and delete the canvas from setupStormWeatherOverlay. Add cheap deck-splash sprites via combatFx (CombatFx already updates at 6297).

### 3. [MAJOR] Weather and rain intensity snap discontinuously at the safe-zone boundary
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:10785
- Why: computeStormRainIntensity returns 0 for dist<=safeRadius and >=0.34 one step outside (10792-10798); computeStormWeatherIntensity caps at 0.24 inside (10782) but starts at 0.52 outside (10777-10779). Crossing the wall by one meter instantly jumps fog density, sun/ambient intensity, exposure (Renderer.updateStormWeather 427-446) and turns rain fully on/off — a hard visual pop instead of sailing into a storm front. Neither value is temporally smoothed (6281 assigns raw).
- Fix: In computeStormWeatherIntensity/computeStormRainIntensity blend across the boundary with smoothstep over ±30m of safeRadius (e.g. edge01 = clamp((dist-safeRadius+30)/60,0,1)) so inside/outside formulas crossfade; then low-pass the result in updateScene: this.stormWeatherIntensity += (target - current) * (1 - Math.exp(-1.5*dt)) before feeding renderer/ocean/audio at 6281-6283.

### 4. [MAJOR] Storm changes shading only — sea geometry and ship rocking are identical inside and outside the storm
- Where: src/client/rendering/OceanRenderer.ts:52
- Why: OCEAN_VERT displaces vertices with u_roughness = time-only shared getOceanRoughness (shared/utils/index.ts:252-256); u_stormIntensity exists only in the fragment shader (OceanRenderer.ts:86) for tinting/fog. Server ship attitude (PhysicsSystem.updateShipWaveAttitude 1424-1470) and client fallback computeWaveMotion (ShipRenderer.ts:2996-3010) sample the same storm-blind field. Result: a 'lethal storm' with a mirror-calm sea and moored hulls sitting motionless — no storm rocking hook exists anywhere.
- Fix: Two layers. Visual (client-only, safe): add u_stormChop uniform to OCEAN_VERT scaling wave amplitude (a *= 1.0 + u_stormChop*0.6) set from setStormIntensity (OceanRenderer.ts:427-430), and give ShipRenderer a setStormIntensity mirroring setNightFactor (Game.ts:6263) that widens computeWaveMotion clamps and adds a slow sway term for ships without server pitch/roll. Authoritative (keeps decks/holes consistent): pass this.state.storm into physics.update (call at Match.ts:794-801), compute per-ship exposure in updateShipWaveAttitude, and scale the pitch/roll targets + windHeel (PhysicsSystem.ts:417-418) by 1+exposure — the replicated ship.pitch/roll/heave then rocks every client identically via ShipRenderer.ts:2571-2577.

### 5. [MAJOR] Elimination cliff: one death force-sinks the whole ship and crew, with a hidden 35m 'outside' margin
- Where: src/server/core/Match.ts:3591
- Why: isShipInStormSafeZone requires the ship to be ≥35m INSIDE the circle (3458-3461), so dying while your ship floats 20m inside the visible wall (fully safe from the DoT in StormSystem.ts:82) is treated as no-respawn → instant elimination; then 3591-3595 force-sinks the still-alive ship and eliminateRemainingCrewAndSinkShip (3499-3524) wipes any other crew. Same cliff in updateRespawns: if the storm edge crosses your drifting ship during the 20s respawn wait, the entire crew is eliminated and the ship destroyed (4197-4204). The storm is designed as gentle DoT (0.6-12 HP/s) but these rules convert boundary proximity into instant crew-wide permadeath the player can neither see (the -35 margin is invisible) nor react to.
- Fix: Make the respawn check match the visible ring (drop the -35 in isShipInStormSafeZone or shrink it to ~5m), and in updateRespawns replace insta-elimination with a hold state: pause respawnTimer while the ship is outside (or keep respawning as long as the ship is alive and let the storm's existing hull DoT decide), only eliminating when the home ship actually sinks (the 4177-4194 branch already handles that correctly). Remove the force-sink at 3591-3595 for storm-caused eliminations — let StormSystem hull damage sink it naturally.

### 6. [MINOR] Storm hull damage bypasses damageHullSection — no holes, no flooding, no feedback until sudden sinking
- Where: src/server/systems/StormSystem.ts:86
- Why: StormSystem writes ship.hull[section] directly (86-88) while every other environmental damage source routes through PhysicsSystem.damageHullSection (e.g. fire at PhysicsSystem.ts:431-437), which is what creates holes/flooding and drives the SoT damage loop. Storm ships therefore silently melt all four sections in lockstep with zero visible damage, no water ingress to bail, and no repair counterplay signal until 'SHIP CRITICAL' (Game.ts:7632-7639) or sinking at avg<=0.18 (Match.ts:2128-2139).
- Fix: Give StormSystem access to the physics helper (pass PhysicsSystem or a callback into storm.update at Match.ts:809) and apply the per-tick ratio to ONE randomized section via damageHullSection so holes spawn and the flooding/bailing loop engages; optionally weight toward the section facing the storm center for readability.

### 7. [MINOR] Endgame HUD shows 'STORM PHASE 8 - NEXT SHRINK 0:00' forever after the final phase
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:7620
- Why: After the last shrink completes, StormSystem sets phase = STORM_PHASES.length (7) and stops (StormSystem.ts:53-59) while shrinkTimer keeps decrementing unboundedly negative (StormSystem.ts:66). The HUD renders phase+1 = 8 (only 7 phases exist) with verb 'NEXT SHRINK' and getStormTimerSeconds clamps the negative timer to 0 (7552-7556), so the top bar promises a shrink at 0:00 that never comes for the rest of the match.
- Fix: In updateHud (7620-7626) special-case this.state.storm.phase >= STORM_PHASES.length: label 'FINAL STORM' / 'STORM AT FULL FURY', hide the timer, and set mapSubtitle to 'The storm holds at its final circle'. Server-side, clamp shrinkTimer to 0 in the else branch of StormSystem.update when phase is terminal.

### 8. [MINOR] Lightning allocates a new PointLight per strike and has no bolt visual or synced thunder
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:10877
- Why: updateLightning constructs a fresh THREE.PointLight every strike and scene-adds/removes it (10877-10884, removal 10847-10851). Changing the scene's light count invalidates lit material programs (first occurrence per count forces shader recompilation — a frame hitch mid-storm) and churns GC. Visually a strike is only a point light + fullscreen soft-light div; there is no bolt geometry, and thunder one-shots in SoundEngine (1025-1040 comment) are ambient-scheduled, not distance-delayed from the visible flash.
- Fix: Create one persistent PointLight at init (next to stormRing setup, 1784-1796) with intensity 0 and reposition/re-energize it per strike so light count never changes; add a 2-3 segment additive billboard bolt mesh (same makeStormTexture canvas trick) flashed for ~120ms at the strike position; trigger audio.playThunder with delay = distanceToCamera/340s from the strike code at 10874-10889.

### 9. [MINOR] No spectate or down-but-not-out: elimination is an instant modal with a frozen corpse camera
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:2272
- Why: On game_over{died:true} the client immediately overlays the death screen (returnToLobbyAfterLoss 2272-2280 via onGameOver 2537-2553); the eliminated Player stays in state.players so updateCamera (7353) leaves the camera parked at the corpse behind the modal. Server has no intermediate state between alive and eliminated (PlayerState union, shared/types/index.ts:116) — hp<=0 resolves the same tick (Match.ts:825-829 → handlePlayerDeath 3526). For a BR this reads abrupt and gives zero social/comeback play; the requested DBNO revive has nowhere to live yet.
- Fix: Add 'downed' to PlayerState plus downedTimer/reviveProgress on Player (shared/types/index.ts:116,145); gate Match.ts:826 to enter downed (crawl-speed, weapons locked, 30s bleed-out) before handlePlayerDeath; see integration_points for the full server/client/bot touch list. Minimum viable alternative if DBNO is deferred: on elimination switch updateCamera to an orbit of the killer or home-ship wreck and delay the modal 4s (kill-cam), keeping the existing death screen as the terminal state.
