# OCEAN subsystem audit (2026-07-04)

## Architecture
OCEAN & WAVES SUBSYSTEM — pirates-br

== Single source of truth ==
h(x,z,t) ALREADY EXISTS and is shared: `gerstnerHeight(x, z, t, WAVE_PARAMS)` at src/shared/utils/index.ts:261-275. It is a sum of 4 vertical-only sine waves (NOT true Gerstner — no horizontal pinch), each amplitude multiplied by `getOceanRoughness(t)` (index.ts:252-256, two slow sines, clamped [0.25, 1.55], periods ~483s and ~1496s). WAVE_PARAMS at index.ts:277-282: {a=0.32, λ=86, spd=5.6}, {0.20, 52, 4.6}, {0.12, 34, 6.5}, {0.06, 20, 7.4}. Max total swell ≈ ±1.08 m at roughness 1.55; ±0.6 m typical; ±0.18 m at the 0.25 floor.

== Client rendering (src/client/rendering/OceanRenderer.ts, the ONLY water surface) ==
- GLSL is code-generated at module load from WAVE_PARAMS (lines 12-35, `WAVE_FIELD_GLSL`): editing WAVE_PARAMS in shared/utils automatically retunes the shader, CPU physics and every gameplay sampler simultaneously. `waveField(p, camDist)` returns (h, dh/dx, dh/dz) with two rendering-only liberties: per-wave camera-distance amplitude fade (fadeStart=9λ, fadeEnd=20λ, line 24-27) and shore damping.
- Vertex shader (50-80): world pos from a translation-only modelMatrix; `v_shoreDamp = mix(0.6, 1.0, smoothstep(-8, 34, shoreDist))` (line 71); displaced `wp.y += waveField(...).x * v_shoreDamp` (73-74). SO THERE IS vertex displacement over time — it is just tiny (steepness k·a ≈ 0.016-0.02 per wave).
- `shoreDist` (37-48): signed distance to nearest island modeled as a CIRCLE `length(p - isl.xy) - isl.z`, from `u_islands[16]`/`u_islandCount` set via `setIslands()` (402-413), fed from Game.ts:2780 with `{x, z, r: i.radius}` (raw roster radius, NOT the real footprint).
- Fragment shader (82-250): analytic per-pixel normal by re-evaluating waveField (136-137); 3-octave scrolling `rippleField` detail normals with distance fade (122-153); trough/flank base color (156-160); turquoise shallows ramp keyed to shoreDist (162-166); Fresnel to horizon color (168-177); Blinn spec with distance-widened lobe (185-193); crest foam from normalized height hn (195-200); animated shore foam band `sin(sd*0.5 - t*1.3)` within sd<17 m plus solid waterline foam sd<3.5 m (202-208); SSS (213-215); night dim (220); storm = color tint + desaturation ONLY (222-225); exp fog + horizon dissolve at 1250-3000 m (227-235); underwater ceiling rendering (237-246). gl_FragColor opaque (248) — no alpha, no depth-based edge blend.
- Geometry (252-307): camera-centered concentric LOD grid, 3 rings sharing one ShaderMaterial; ring extents/cells per quality (LOD_LEVELS 258-274): low ~13k verts, balanced ~29k, high ~117k; outermost halfExtent 3264 m (world is WORLD.HALF=1000, shared/constants/index.ts:6). Grid group snaps to the coarsest cell (64/96 m) around the camera (386-388) so vertices stay on a fixed world lattice. `frustumCulled=false`, `receiveShadow=false` (355-356), `side: DoubleSide` (348). Plus one 2400×2400 MeshBasicMaterial underlayer at y=-1.6 hiding LOD T-junction cracks (363-370). Total: 4 draw calls, 2 materials.
- Clock: `update(dt)` (380-389) advances `this.time`, sets u_time and u_roughness=getOceanRoughness(time); `setWaveTime(t)` (394-400) snaps the clock.

== Clock sync / driving loop (src/client/core/Game.ts) ==
frame() at 5957: `serverTimeOffset` estimated per snapshot as `snapshot.serverTime - performance.now()/1000`, EMA gain 0.1 (2738-2742; serverTime is match-relative `this.t`, Match.ts:735,740,3674). Every frame: `ocean.setWaveTime(perf.now/1000 + offset)` (5968), then `ocean.update(dt, camera.position)` (5970 — adds one extra dt; constant ~16 ms bias shared by all consumers), then `ocean.setAtmosphere(renderer.getAtmosphere())` (5971; Atmosphere = sunDir/fogColor/horizonColor/storminess/nightFactor, Renderer.ts:165-171, 511-521). ALL gameplay/visual wave sampling on the client uses `this.ocean.getTime()` as t (e.g. 6269, 6454, 7505), so client consumers are mutually consistent.

== Storm ==
Server StormSystem.ts is purely the battle-royale shrink ring (safeRadius/center/damage; no sea-state anywhere in its 117 lines). Client `computeStormWeatherIntensity()` (Game.ts:10761-10780) is LOCAL-PLAYER-RELATIVE (distance to storm edge + phase boost) and feeds `ocean.setStormIntensity` (6283) → u_stormIntensity → fragment tint/spec/foam boost only. Wave GEOMETRY is never storm-affected on either side: u_roughness comes solely from getOceanRoughness(t) (OceanRenderer.ts:383,398) and gerstnerHeight uses the same.

== Ship floating height / attitude ==
SERVER (PhysicsSystem.ts): buoyancy at 386-397 — spring-damper heave toward `buoyTarget = gerstnerHeight(ship.x, ship.z, t) - waterLevel*FREEBOARD_DROP` (391-396); residual published as `ship.heave = clamp(buoyTarget - position.y, -2, 2)` (397). Attitude at updateShipWaveAttitude (1424-1465): samples gerstnerHeight at bow/stern/port/starboard (1438-1441), spring-chases pitch/roll with speed trim + turn heel + windHeel. Flooding waterline check also samples per-section gerstnerHeight (152-173). Players: water surface = waveY+0.32 (541-542, 772-773), island submersion (697), projectile water hit (972). Match.ts raymarch splash vs real surface (2832-2855).
CLIENT (ShipRenderer.ts): update() 2522+ — `waveT = waveTimeOverride ?? t` (2525; setWaveTime at 860-861 is NEVER called, so waveT = ocean clock passed at Game.ts:6269). Prefers server pitch/roll/heave from snapshot (2571-2577), fallback `computeWaveMotion` sampling gerstnerHeight at 4 hull points (2996-3009). Visual y = `ship.position.y + heave` lerped (2582-2592). Wake ribbon and bow spray sit ON the shared field (2952, 2969). Swimmer visual snap to `gerstnerHeight(...)+0.28` (Game.ts:6455-6467; server uses +0.32). Camera underwater depth from gerstnerHeight at camera xz (Game.ts:7503-7509) → Renderer.updateWaterEnvironment (Renderer.ts:448+) + ocean.setUnderwaterDepth.

== Island coast data available for shoreline foam ==
shared/utils/index.ts: `getIslandCoastWeights(island, angle)` → {beach, rocky, cliff} (375-392), `getIslandCoastField` (365-371), `getIslandInletCut` (307-320), `getIslandDistRatio(island, x, z)` → {angle, distRatio, bulge} where distRatio==1 is the true waterline (338-347), shape terms with footprintX/Z ∈ [1.0, 2.0] and bulge (322-336; footprint ranges MapGenerator.ts:251-299). The client already consumes these for terrain vertex colors at Game.ts:3088-3106. True waterline radius along direction θ: `radius * bulge(θ) * fx*fz / sqrt((fz·cosθ)² + (fx·sinθ)²)` — bakeable per island into a small DataTexture (e.g. 128 angles × 16 islands, 2 channels: rEff + beach weight). WORLD.ISLAND_COUNT=10 ≤ MAX_ISLANDS=16 uniform slots.

== Perf constraints ==
Fragment cost dominates: waveField re-eval (4 sin + 4 cos) + shoreDist 16-iter loop run in BOTH vert and frag, + 9 value-noise evals for ripples, over a full-screen 117k-vert surface at high quality. Each added WAVE_PARAMS entry costs vertex AND fragment ALU (the GLSL is unrolled per wave). Keep island uniform loop bounded (u_islandCount early-break exists). Draw calls fixed at 4; one shared material — per-island materials would break this.

## Findings

### 1. [MAJOR] Shore foam/shallows/damping keyed to a circular island of raw roster radius — misses the real coastline by 10s of meters
- Where: src/client/rendering/OceanRenderer.ts:39
- Why: shoreDist (OceanRenderer.ts:39-47) models each island as a circle of radius u_islands[i].z, fed `r: i.radius` at Game.ts:2780. But the actual waterline sits at radius x footprintX/Z (1.0-2.0, MapGenerator.ts:251-299) x bulge(angle) (+-40% ridge/hills, up to -60% at inlets) per getIslandShapeTerms (shared/utils/index.ts:322-336). E.g. Old Maw Caldera (radius 112, footprint up to 2.0) has its beach at ~180-220 m while the foam band (frag 202-208), waterline foam (206), turquoise shallows (163-166) and vertex shore damping (71) all render at ~112 m — deep underwater, nowhere near the sand. This is exactly the screenshot symptom: beach meets water with a hard seam and no visible foam/blend.
- Fix: Bake the true angular waterline per island client-side: rEff(theta) = island.radius * bulge(theta) * fx*fz / sqrt((fz*cos)^2 + (fx*sin)^2) using getIslandShapeTerms/getIslandInletCut from shared/utils (322-347), sampled at ~128 angles per island into a 128x16 RG float DataTexture (R = rEff, G = beach weight from getIslandCoastWeights at index.ts:375). In OceanRenderer replace the SHORE_GLSL circle with: theta = atan(p.y - isl.y, p.x - isl.x); r = texture(u_coastTex, vec2(theta/2pi, islandIndex)); d = length(p - isl.xy) - r.x. Build the texture where Game.ts:2780 currently calls setIslands (it already has state.islands with full profiles). Gate foam band strength by the sampled beach weight so cliffs get spray, beaches get lapping foam.

### 2. [MAJOR] Waves are near-invisible by construction: ~2% steepness, vertical-only sines, and a roughness floor that flattens the sea for minutes
- Where: src/shared/utils/index.ts:277
- Why: WAVE_PARAMS (index.ts:277-282) give per-wave steepness k*a*roughness of only 0.016-0.02 (e.g. 2pi/86 * 0.32 * 0.85), and the displacement is vertical-only (gerstnerHeight index.ts:261-275; GLSL mirrors it, OceanRenderer.ts:26-31) with no horizontal crest sharpening. getOceanRoughness (index.ts:252-256) clamps at a 0.25 floor, and its two sines (periods ~483 s and ~1496 s) can hold it AT that floor for minutes — total swell +-0.18 m. From any gameplay camera the surface reads as a static sparkle plane (the rippleField normals scroll, geometry visibly doesn't), matching the screenshot.
- Fix: Retune WAVE_PARAMS in shared/utils/index.ts:277-282 (single edit propagates to GLSL, server physics, ship rocking automatically): raise amplitudes toward steepness 0.05-0.08 (e.g. 0.85@86m, 0.55@52m, 0.35@34m, 0.18@20m) and add one 12-14 m chop wave; raise the roughness clamp floor in getOceanRoughness (index.ts:255) from 0.25 to ~0.55 so 'calm' is still alive. Verify BUOYANCY_SPRING/HEAVE_DAMPING (PhysicsSystem.ts:395) and the client attitude clamps (ShipRenderer.ts:3005-3007, +-0.05/0.06 rad) still track the bigger field — the client fallback clamps will saturate and should be loosened toward the server's +-0.35/0.45 (PhysicsSystem.ts:1451,1456).

### 3. [MAJOR] Storm never changes the sea state — geometry identical in dead calm and hurricane, only a color tint applies
- Where: src/client/rendering/OceanRenderer.ts:383
- Why: u_roughness is set exclusively from getOceanRoughness(time) (OceanRenderer.ts:383, 398), which has no storm input; u_stormIntensity only darkens/desaturates color, widens the spec lobe, and boosts foam in the fragment shader (166, 187, 196, 222-235). Server-side gerstnerHeight likewise ignores StormState (StormSystem.ts contains zero wave logic). Screenshots inside a storm therefore show the same flat water with a gray filter — a big believability gap for a pirate battle royale.
- Fix: Add a shared, deterministic storm-sea factor derived from snapshot storm state that both sides already have (phase + shrinkProgress — NOT the client-local computeStormWeatherIntensity at Game.ts:10761, which depends on the local player's position and would desync physics). E.g. export getStormSeaFactor(storm) in shared/utils and change gerstnerHeight/getOceanRoughness to accept an optional multiplier; server passes it in PhysicsSystem/Match call sites, client stores it on Game and passes via a new OceanRenderer.setSeaState(mult) that multiplies into u_roughness at OceanRenderer.ts:383/398 and into ShipRenderer/Game gerstnerHeight calls. Keep the multiplier global (world-wide sea state), not distance-to-storm-edge, so h(x,z,t) stays identical on client and server.

### 4. [MINOR] Render-only shore damping (x0.6) diverges from the physics wave field — hulls and swimmers bob above/below the drawn surface near shore
- Where: src/client/rendering/OceanRenderer.ts:71
- Why: The vertex shader damps displacement to 60-100% within ~34 m of an island (v_shoreDamp, OceanRenderer.ts:71,73), but every CPU consumer — server buoyancy (PhysicsSystem.ts:391), attitude (1438-1441), swimmer surface (541, 772), client wake/spray/heave fallback (ShipRenderer.ts:2952, 2999-3002), swim snap (Game.ts:6455) — samples the UNDAMPED gerstnerHeight. Near docks and beaches (exactly where players moor and swim) the entity rides up to 0.4x the wave height (~0.25 m typical) off the visible surface, so moored ships hover/clip at crests and swimmers float above the water. The header comment (lines 7-11) acknowledges the liberty but underestimates its visibility at the beach seam.
- Fix: Make the damping shared: export shoreWaveDamp(x, z, islands) in shared/utils (same mix(0.6,1,smoothstep(-8,34,d)) shape, using the corrected per-angle coast radius from finding 1) and multiply it inside gerstnerHeight when an optional islands arg is provided; PhysicsSystem/Match already hold islands, ShipRenderer/Game have state.islands. Alternatively (cheaper) raise the GLSL damp floor from 0.6 to ~0.85 at OceanRenderer.ts:71 to shrink the max divergence to ~0.09 m.

### 5. [MINOR] ShipRenderer.setWaveTime override is dead plumbing that invites a second wave clock
- Where: src/client/rendering/ShipRenderer.ts:860
- Why: waveTimeOverride (ShipRenderer.ts:842, 860-861, consumed at 2525) is never set from anywhere (only OceanRenderer.setWaveTime is called, Game.ts:5968); the ships already receive the ocean clock as `t` (Game.ts:6269). A future caller passing a different clock here would silently desync ship rocking, wakes, and spray (2952, 2969, 2999-3002) from the drawn ocean surface, which is the exact class of bug this audit is chasing.
- Fix: Delete waveTimeOverride and setWaveTime from ShipRenderer (842, 860-861, 2525) and use the `t` parameter directly, or have Game.ts call it with this.ocean.getTime() explicitly and drop the `?? t` fallback — one clock, one owner (the OceanRenderer time snapped at Game.ts:5967-5970).
