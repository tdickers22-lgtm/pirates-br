## Findings

### 1. [MAJOR] Projectiles die on the water surface before the ship-hull test runs — waterline shots can never hole a hull
- Where: src/server/systems/PhysicsSystem.ts:972
- Why: updateProjectiles checks `proj.position.y < gerstnerHeight(...)` (972-975) BEFORE the ship loop (977-985). The hull volume extends |y−ship.position.y|<H+1.1 (1854-1859) and ships float AT waveY (buoyTarget=waveY, 394), so any shot whose post-step sample sits below the local wave — exactly the classic aim-at-the-waterline shot the flooding model rewards, and any hit while a crest covers the impact point (Gerstner swings ~±1m, 60m/s ball moves ~1m/tick) — is killed as a splash with zero hull damage.
- Fix: Reorder: run the `isProjectileInsideShipHull` loop before the water kill in updateProjectiles (move lines 977-985 above 971-975), or make the water check `if (below surface && !ships.some(s => this.isProjectileInsideShipHull(proj, s)))`. Keep the bullet early-return in onProjectileHitShip (1047) so small arms still splash.

### 2. [MAJOR] No impact-point hull damage visuals; ship_hit is attacker-only so victims/bystanders can never get them
- Where: src/server/core/Match.ts:3712
- Why: relayPendingCombatEvents sends ship_hit only via notifyShipHit(event.attackerId,…) (3712, 3788-3808 sends to one client). The client's only hole visual is one fixed decal per section driven by hull HP (ShipRenderer.ts:2759-2766); the impact `position` in the event (PhysicsSystem.ts:1071-1083) is discarded for everyone except the attacker's hit-confirm spark (Game.ts:2646-2648). ShipMeshEntry.damageMeshes is declared and initialised empty but never populated (ShipRenderer.ts:809, 2427) — the per-impact decal system was stubbed and never built. Result: crews see no holes punched where they were hit, no breach callout, no punch-through FX.
- Fix: In relayPendingCombatEvents (Match.ts:3710-3722) broadcast a slim `ship_damage {targetId, section, position, remainingSection}` to all clients (or at least the target crew) alongside the attacker-only confirm. Client: new NetworkClient handler → Game.ts → `shipRenderer.addHullDamage(shipId, section, worldPos)` that converts to ship-local (root.worldToLocal), projects onto the hull surface, spawns a pooled hole decal into damageMeshes, plus a CombatFx wood burst; clear decals for a section when hull[section] rises past 0.9 (repair edge, detectable in the existing hullSections loop at ShipRenderer.ts:2759).

### 3. [MAJOR] Hole decals are placed on a box approximation, so they sink into or float off the real tapered hull
- Where: src/client/rendering/ShipRenderer.ts:1094
- Why: addHole places flat circles at x=±(W·0.5+0.018), y=H·0.42, z=0 and z=±(L·0.5+0.018) (1094-1098), but the actual hull from makeHullGeometry (165-223) tapers per station: for a sloop (W=5) the topside surface at the decal's y/z runs x≈2.46-2.57 while the decal plane sits at 2.518 — buried ~5cm inside the planking over the aft half of its own span and floating up to ~12cm in the air over the forward half (worse at scale 1.45). The bow decal (circle r=0.4-0.58) hovers around a stem only ~0.15-0.3 half-width at that height (station 5: half=W·0.055, 172). So 'holes' either z-fight, hide inside the hull, or hang in space beside it.
- Fix: Compute the true surface point/normal from the same station table: interpolate stations by z, then lerp chine→deck by y to get x_surface and the topside slope; set hole position to that point + tiny normal offset and rotate the group to the face normal (add rotation.z tilt for the topside flare). Give holeMat/rim `polygonOffset: true, polygonOffsetFactor: -1` to kill residual z-fighting. Move bow/stern holes onto the end-cap dimensions (closeStation faces, 204-215) or drop them to the wider chine height.

### 4. [MAJOR] Flooded ships never list — no waterLevel/section term in server attitude or client roll
- Where: src/server/systems/PhysicsSystem.ts:1454
- Why: updateShipWaveAttitude's targetPitch/targetRoll (1449-1457) are wave slope + speed trim + turn heel + windHeel only; updateShipFlooding never touches pitch/roll; the client rollTarget (ShipRenderer.ts:2640-2643) is wave+steer+sail lean and otherwise mirrors server roll (2571-2577). FREEBOARD_DROP (394) sinks the whole hull evenly, so a port-holed, half-swamped ship sits level — the 'ship lists, then sinks' beat is missing entirely, and players get no readable cue of WHICH side is holed.
- Fix: In updateShipWaveAttitude, reuse the exported evaluateSectionFlood(ship, t): add rollBias = clamp(water · 0.35 · (Σ flooding rails: starboard +1, port −1) …, ±0.2) and pitchBias for bow(+)/stern(−) into targetRoll/targetPitch before the spring (1461-1464); clamps at 1463-1464 already bound it. Client picks it up automatically via serverRoll/serverPitch. Keep the heel test in scripts/test-flooding.mjs green (it sets roll directly, doesn't run attitude).

### 5. [MAJOR] Rendered hull has zero draft (keel at the waterline) — 'hole below the waterline' is undrawable and hulls read as slabs sitting on the sea
- Where: src/client/rendering/ShipRenderer.ts:165
- Why: makeHullGeometry keel y is ≥0 (station keelY min 0, 167-172) and the mesh is added un-offset (1044-1048) under a root positioned at ship.position.y+heave (2583-2592), while the server floats ship.position.y AT waveY (PhysicsSystem.ts:394) and gameplay assumes a keel 0.55·H BELOW that (KEEL_DRAFT_RATIO grounding, PhysicsSystem.ts:1595; swim band −0.72·H, shared/utils:868-873). So the entire hull renders above the water: the server's flood holes ('punched near/below the static float line', holeY≈position.y, PhysicsSystem.ts:167) sit at the rendered KEEL, hull leak FX stream from the hull's bottom edge, and no hull surface exists below the waterline for a breach to read against — matching the 'flat vertical slab' screenshots.
- Fix: Give the visual hull draft: extend makeHullGeometry stations below y=0 (keel ≈ −0.55·H·(bilge fullness) to match KEEL_DRAFT_RATIO) with a distinct below-waterline paint band, keeping deck at H so player placement (Match.ts:4305) is untouched; alternatively shift only the hull mesh down and re-anchor holes/wales. Then relocate the section hole decals to the new waterline row (y≈0 local) so punched holes visibly straddle the water, consistent with emitHullLeaks.

### 6. [MAJOR] Chainshot cannot hit the rigging it is designed to shred — hull-band-only hit test
- Where: src/server/systems/PhysicsSystem.ts:1857
- Why: isProjectileInsideShipHull requires |proj.y − ship.position.y| < stats.height + 1.1 (sloop: 3.3m). Masts and canvas extend far above that, so a chainshot aimed through the sails passes clean through and only registers when it strikes the hull band — yet its whole effect is sail damage (onProjectileHitShip 1089-1098). The client even fakes it: any chainshot dying above y=0.9 plays a sail-rip sound (Game.ts:6728-6736) the server never registered.
- Fix: In the ship-hit loop (977-985), special-case proj.type==='chainshot': accept a taller band (ship.position.y .. +stats.height + mastHeight, e.g. stats.length·0.9) with a narrower footprint (mast/sail plane near getMainMastLocalZ(stats) from shared/utils, half-width ≈ stats.width·0.6). onProjectileHitShip already deals 0 hull damage for chainshot so no other change is needed; add mast-height per type next to SHIP_STATS (constants:64-86) so client/renderer agree.

### 7. [MINOR] floodingRate ignores bailing, so the water-gauge trend arrow shows 'rising' while bailers are winning
- Where: src/server/systems/PhysicsSystem.ts:196
- Why: Bailing subtracts water earlier in the tick (Match.ts:1197, 2167), then updateShipFlooding overwrites floodingRate = raw ingress (196) whenever any hole is open. With 1 bailer vs 1 hole (bail 0.014 > ingress 0.010) waterLevel falls every tick but the HUD trend (Game.ts:8987-8998) shows ▲ red — the core feedback for 'is my bucket work helping?' is inverted.
- Fix: Make floodingRate the net delta: in Match.update record each ship's waterLevel at tick start and after physics set ship.floodingRate = (now − before)/dt; or pass bailedRate into updateShipFlooding (Match tracks bailers per ship) and publish ingress − bailedRate. Update the exact-pump assertion in scripts/test-flooding.mjs:176-181 if the trend semantics change.

### 8. [MINOR] Waterline hull hits render as water splashes — fixed WATER_IMPACT_THRESHOLD_Y
- Where: src/client/rendering/CombatFx.ts:6
- Why: emitImpact classifies anything below y=0.9 as a water hit (841-847). Ships float at waveY (~±1m) with hull bottoms at that height, so cannonballs striking the lower hull — precisely the flooding-relevant hits — draw a splash instead of the flame/sparks/wood-chip burst (854-888). Together with the server-side water-first kill this leaves the waterline with zero 'wood punched' feedback.
- Fix: Classify in Game.syncProjectiles (6718-6743) where state is available: water only if position.y < gerstnerHeight(x, z, ocean.getTime(), WAVE_PARAMS) − 0.15 (already imported at Game.ts:6) AND the point is not inside any ship footprint (isInsideSwimHullFootprint + vertical band, both imported); pass an explicit `onWater` flag into emitImpact instead of the y constant.

### 9. [MINOR] Leak FX and hole decals disagree by ~0.75m of height
- Where: src/client/core/Game.ts:8949
- Why: emitHullLeaks streams droplets at worldY offset 0.16 above ship.position.y (8949, getShipWorldPoint 10143-10151) — the float line — while the visible hole decal for the same section sits at local y=H·0.42 (~0.92m on a sloop, ShipRenderer.ts:1095-1098). Water appears to pour out of intact planking below the hole.
- Fix: Single source the hole anchor: export a getHullHoleLocalPoint(type, section) from ShipRenderer (or shared constants) used both by addHole placement and emitHullLeaks; once holes move to the waterline row (draft fix), 0.16 becomes correct and the decal should follow it.

### 10. [MINOR] 'Cannonball' ammo selection has no fallback, unlike firebomb/chainshot selections
- Where: src/server/systems/WeaponSystem.ts:222
- Why: fireShipCannon: preferred 'firebomb' falls back chainshot→cannonball (196-208), preferred 'chainshot' falls back firebomb→cannonball (209-221), but the default 'cannonball' branch (222-228) only checks cannonballIdx — with 0 cannonballs and a hold full of firebombs/chainshot the cannon silently refuses to fire (231) unless a super shot is banked.
- Fix: Mirror the fallback: in the else branch try cannonballIdx, then firebombIdx (projType='firebomb'), then chainshotIdx (projType='chainshot'), keeping usesSuperShot's cannonball-only gate at 230 intact.

### 11. [MINOR] Bots bail and plug holes with none of the player constraints (any station, any distance, every bot at once)
- Where: src/server/core/Match.ts:2146
- Why: updateBotFlooding lets every non-eliminated bot on its ship bail at full BAIL_RATE while simultaneously helming/gunning (no atHelm/atCannon/atSails exclusion, unlike the player gate at 1191-1194) and repair the weakest holed section from anywhere on deck (2158-2162), while players must physically stand at the section rail (getRepairableHullSection 4319-4324). A 3-4 bot crew gets ~0.042-0.056/s of free drain plus instant hole plugging — player ships flood effectively faster than bot ships.
- Fix: In updateBotFlooding skip bots with atHelm/atCannon/atSails/atCrowNest set, cap active bailers (e.g. crew.length−1), and gate the plank repair on a proximity check reusing getRepairableHullSection's rail distance (or walk the bot there via BotSystem before repairing).

### 12. [MINOR] Ramming a ship to death never credits the attacker — crew survive and no kill is banked
- Where: src/server/systems/PhysicsSystem.ts:1559
- Why: resolveShipShipCollision applies hull damage directly (1559-1573) without pushing a combat event, and markShipDamagedByPlayer is only fed by projectile ship_hit relays (Match.ts:3711) and kegs (1941). A ram-sink reaches evaluateShipSinking with no shipLastDamagedByPlayer entry → getRecentShipSinkAttackerId returns null (3357-3363) → startShipSinking skips eliminateCrewForShipSink (3294-3297): victims respawn-swim and the rammer gets nothing, unlike every other sink route.
- Fix: Emit a ship_hit-style combatEvent from resolveShipShipCollision (attackerId = other ship's helmsman or ownerId, damage = otherDmg·maxHull) so relayPendingCombatEvents (3710-3722) records credit; or have Match diff hull totals after physics.update and call markShipDamagedByPlayer(ship.id, otherShip.ownerId) when collision damage occurred.
